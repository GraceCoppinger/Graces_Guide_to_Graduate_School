Life in Graduate School

Everything you need to know about graduate school.

Preface

Each program even within Universities is different. Here is a rough breakdown of how mine works.

WashU EEB Program

First year

If your program deos rotations, you will rotate through 3-4 labs (10 weeks at a time). You will also spend your first year taking classes. I took Community ecology, Macroevolution, Disease ecology, and also a “journal club” class for 1st-2nd year graduate students to practice presenting on papers every week. Spend time discovering what lab you want to join/what questions you want to ask Read more papers then you ever have in your whole life

Second year

Take more classes for programs. Start your lab research. Mostly pilot studies to figure out what your overall research will be. Take your qualifying exam.

The qualifying exam

A qualifying exam (QE) basically determines if you have learned enough about your program (in my case ecology and evolution) broadly to move onto narrower research topics. For some programs/schools the QE (often called a "prelim" or "comps") is a major milestone that transitions a PhD student from coursework into official candidacy for their doctorate. For WashU you have three faculty members. You present a brief 30 min presentation on a topic of your choosing (I did the biodiversity-disease debate), and then they can ask you any questions they want for 1.5 hours. Do not worry. WHile this is a big exam and you should prepare for it accordingly, many programs have intensive prep classes or other activities to help you prepare.

Third year

Do you proposal defense, and continue research.

The proposal

The proposal is where you write up a document intensively detailing everything you plan to study and why. You do also have to do a defense where you present to your thesis committee. Completing the proposal takes you from a graduate student to a grduate canidate.

The thesis committee

A thesis committee is 4-6 faculty members of your choosing to help you along your PhD process. Your PI is one of them, but you also have to pick a chair (not your PI). The chair is someone who you should be very close to as they will help guide your research projects and carer goals a lot. These committee members will stick with you throughout your P.h.D and meet with you twice a year to discuss your project.

Fourth year and beyon

Continue research until you are ready to do your thesis defense. You comittee will determine if you are ready during a final permission to defend meeting.

The thesis defense

Along with a written thesis, you will given a defense. In the thesis defense you have an hour to showcase all the work you did and key findings. Then you will meet with your committee for roughly an hour to answer any questions and show you are deserving of your degree!
