About This Blog

Applying to graduate school can feel overwhelming. From finding the right advisor to navigating applications, funding, and the transition to graduate student life, there is a lot to figure out.

I created this guide to share advice, resources, and lessons I've learned along the way. Whether you're just beginning to consider graduate school or preparing to submit applications, I hope you find something here that helps you take the next step.
