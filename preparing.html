Preparing for Graduate School

What you can do to prepare for graduate school.

Reading Scientific Papers

There are many ways to find scientific papers, but I prefer to use Google Scholar as a search engine. Papers are published in different journals, and each journal is different in terms of its academic impact. Journals like Nature, Cell, and Science have very high impact, and papers published here are highly regarded. Don't only read from those journals, but be aware that there are different levels of credibility. This is because each journal has its own standards for peer review. I really recommend focusing on more recently published papers (2020–2025), since the wording will be more friendly.

Reading Popular Science Books

Something that has been immensely helpful to me in graduate school has been reading popular science books. I like to think of them as a 300-page textbook on a niche subject. It is a great way to gain insight into new fields and expand your knowledge to pull from in conversations. During my qualifying exam, for example, I was constantly using references I had actually read about in my popular science books! I feel like they have made me a much more well-rounded scientist and have greatly improved my conversation skills!
