Preparing for Graduate School

What you can do to prepare for graduate school.

Reading scientific papers

There are many ways to find scientific papers, but I prefer to use google scholar as a search engine. Papers are published in different journals and each journal is different in terms of its academic impact. Journals like Nature, Cell, and Science have very high impacts and papers published here are highly regarded. Don't only read from those journals but just be aware there are different levels of credibility. This is because each journal has its own metric for peer review. I really recommend focusing on more recent published papers (2020-2025) since the wording will be more friendly.

Journals I really like: Nature Ecology and Evolution American Naturalist Frontiers in Plant Science

Reading popular science books

Something that has been immensely helpful to me in graduate school has been reading popular science books. I like to think of them as a 300 page textbook on a niche subject. It is a great way to gain insight to new fields and expand your knowledge to pull from in conversations. During my qualifying exam for example I was constantly using references I actually had read about in my popular science books! I feel like they have made me a much more well rounded scientist and have greatly improved my conversation skills!

Graces favorite popular science books

Sea of Grass: The Conquest, Ruin, and Redemption of Nature on the American Prairie Sea of Grass Crossings: How Road Ecology Is Shaping the Future of Our Planet Crossings The Catalyst: RNA and the Quest to Unlock Life's Deepest Secrets The Catalyst
