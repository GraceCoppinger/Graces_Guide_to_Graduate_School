Fellowships

Getting a fellowship for graduate school

The NSF-GRFP

The GRFP is a very prestigious grant given to graduate students that pays for a portion of your school. The five-year Fellowship provides three years of financial support (for your research), including an annual stipend of $37,000 (just for you).
